Key Features

Everything a small warehouse actually needs

Six focused modules, all working together on one live inventory.

Product management

Add, edit and delete products with SKU, category, price, rack location and minimum stock level.

Stock In management

Record incoming deliveries against a purchase reference and see quantities increase instantly.

Stock Out management

Log dispatches and orders. StockFlow blocks any outgoing quantity that exceeds what you hold.

Inventory tracking

Search and filter by category or status, and review a full history of every stock movement.

Low-stock alerts

Products at or below their minimum level are flagged right away, with one-click restocking.

Inventory analytics

Monthly stock-in vs stock-out charts, stock health breakdown and performance statistics.
